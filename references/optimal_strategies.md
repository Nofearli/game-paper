# Optimal Strategies — Design Patterns from the Library

Source: per-paper archives `papers/PXXX.md` (Sections 4–5), synthesized 2026-10-11. Strategy classes are ordered from geometry-first to learning-first. For each pattern: **input/output** (what state information it consumes, what it emits), **information requirements**, **centralized vs distributed**, **guarantee level** (saddle-point proof / necessary conditions only / heuristic), and exemplars with page/eq as recorded in the archives. **[proven]** = archive records a proof; **[asserted]** = authors' claim with a flagged gap. Guarantee levels: **SP** = state-feedback saddle-point/Nash equilibrium with value verification; **NC** = necessary conditions only (PMP); **FWD** = forward sufficient certificate (winning/capture guarantee without a value function); **H** = heuristic/learning, no formal guarantee.

## 1. Apollonius-based point-capture and aimpoint strategies

- **Pattern (aim at the dominant-region point closest to the goal).** Input: all players' positions, speeds, capture radii. Output: per-pursuer heading toward the unique point $x_I$ of the evader's dominance region nearest $\Omega_{\text{goal}}$. Information: full state. Centralized construction, distributed execution (each pursuer needs only the shared aimpoint). Guarantee: **FWD→SP** — P104 Sec. 6.2/6.3: since the dominance region is strictly convex (faster pursuers), the closest point to a disjoint convex goal is unique and heading there guarantees pursuit winning [proven in Yan et al. 2019b/2022]; the convex-program form (P104 eq. (19)) `min ‖x−y‖ s.t. f_ij(x) ≥ 0, g(y) ≤ 0` extends to $v_{P_i} \ge v_{E_j}$ with $r_i \ge 0$ where closed forms fail. Singularity caveat recorded: non-convex goal regions break uniqueness (P104 Sec. 6.2, citing Von Moll et al. 2020).
- **Evasion-space (ES) interception points** (P103 Sec. 4; P058 Sec. III). Input: coalition positions/radii + evader state. Output: optimal interception point (OIP) on the generalized Apollonius surface (zero level set of $f_{ij}$); three classes of interception points for two-stage CTF play ($I_{F0}, I_R, I_{R0}$, P058). Guarantee: **SP for subgames** — ES strict convexity (P058 Lemma 1 [proven]) + straight-line optimality (Lemma 2) give necessary-and-sufficient game-of-kind conditions (P058 Theorems 1–2 [proven]).
- **Optimal target point (OTP) machinery.** P100 Lemma 1 (monotony of $G_1$, stationarity eq. (6)) makes the evader's best crossing point unique [proven]; P102's OTP construction (Theorems 2–3) does the same with radius $l$ and lifetime; P012's OTP is "the feasible point of smallest y-coordinate in the reaching region; on the barrier the OTP lies exactly on $\mathcal{T}$" (contribution 2).
- **Containment variant (Lyapunov, no aimpoint).** P023 Theorem 1: pursue the AC-deflated point $\mathbf{v}_P = \hat{\mathbf{z}}_P$, $\mathbf{z}_P = (R_C - R_A)\hat{\mathbf{r}} + \nu\mathbf{y}$; the time-varying AC stays strictly inside the fixed disc $\mathcal{C}$ (initial AC inflated by $\delta$) and capture occurs by $t \le t_0 + 2(1+\nu^{-1})R_C\ln(R_C/\delta)$ [proven]; Corollary 2 ⇒ a pursuer with sensor range $\rho \ge \|r(t_0)\|$ retains detection forever. Information: own+opponent positions only; the law is feedback; no opponent model. This pattern "dissolves the dispersal/switching-surface pathologies" of HJI treatments (P023 [CITATION]).
- **Area-rate min–max pursuit.** P066: $\min_{\{u_i\}} \max_v \dot{A}_e(t)$ over the area of the evader's safe-reachable set $S_e = \bigcap_i B_i$ (Apollonius discs); value $\dot{A}_e = V_e\|F_e\| - \sum_i V_i\|F_{p_i}\|$ (eq. (10)); gradients of the disc intersection computed without closed-form area via a Leibniz/Reynolds rule (Prop. 1.3, Theorem 1.4) [proven]. Distributed inputs but global geometry (arcs/centroids) — effectively centralized. Guarantee: **H/FWD** (instantaneous min–max, no capture-time theorem recorded).
- **Voronoi/area-gradient pursuit (equal speeds).** P113 Eq. (6): $u_i^* = -(\partial A/\partial x_{p^i})/\|\partial A/\partial x_{p^i}\|$; Theorem 2: head to the midpoint of the line of control [proven]; Theorem 3: energy $E = kA + \|x_e - x_p\|^2$ decreases linearly ⇒ finite-time capture, $t_{cap} \le E(0)/c(\beta)$ [proven for $N = 1$, convex Ω; $N>1$ reduction asserted; case-dichotomy typo "ż > c(β)" flagged in archive]. Decentralized: inputs decouple given the shared partition (positions only). Safe-reachable-set extension (Sec. 4): modified FMM, receding-horizon feedback; **no capture theorem in the non-convex case** (author-stated, p. 69); slower pursuers break it ($S_e$ can grow, Fig. 3).

## 2. Dominance-region heading strategies with switching (stay/redirect rules)

- **Sign / side-of-barrier strategies (perimeter defense).** P083 Item 3: defender equilibrium $\omega_D^* = \mathrm{sign}(\theta)$ (move toward the intruder's side of the front); intruder runs to the tangent point of the ν-scaled circle. Arbitrary convex perimeter: approach angle $\varphi = \cos^{-1}\nu$ (Item 4); involute-based barrier at $\nu = 1$ (Item 5) [asserted — from the self-review's own chain]. Guarantee: **SP** for the 1v1 game of degree (barrier = level set $V = 0$, P083 Item 2), with the pincer 2v1 winning region strictly larger than the union of individual regions (Item 6) [asserted, citing [25,28,32]].
- **EDRC (evader-dominance-region-compression) pursuit.** P101 Lemma 3.1/Theorem 3.2: run toward the intersection of the current Apollonius circle $\mathcal{A}_i$ with the ray from $E_i$ along $E_i$'s current heading; the new AC is internally tangent to the old one, so $E_i$'s dominance region strictly shrinks until capture; unique closed-form feedback realization (eq. (11)) [proven]. Input: own + target evader states (and current evader heading). Guarantee: **FWD** (capture guarantee, not a value).
- **Apollonius-point pursuit under sensing limits.** P064 Lemma 1: head straight to the optimal Apollonius point; provably keeps the evader inside the sensing disk iff $\alpha_{ij} \ge \sqrt{2}$ [asserted — "optimality under exact velocity conditions" caveat]; the conservative barrier is radius-free, ratio-only, evaluated at sensing-range inflation (contribution 2). P067: deployment rule — guard the target-line midpoint $(l/2, 0)$; phase transition at $R^\alpha = (1 + \alpha^{-1})l/2$ between guarding and retreating to barrier-tangency depth [proven via convexity, Theorem 1].
- **Time-shifted dominance for sequential capture.** P101 Def. 4.1 δ-Apollonius circle $\mathcal{A}_i^\delta$ (locus reachable by P exactly δ earlier), Lemma 4.2 explicit polar form (eqs. (18)–(23)), Theorem 4.3 time-shifted barrier and winning regions [proven]; P104 Sec. 4.4 recaps the same object as $\mathbb{E}^\delta_j$ with the aiding/sacrifice equilibrium: the first-pursued evader moves away from the goal to improve the second's escape position ("prey-like" behavior; order comparison shows P should pursue $E_1$ first) [asserted, authors' conclusion].

## 3. Barrier-based switching strategies (explicit-policy method)

- **Construction recipe** (P087; P056; P112): (1) split the game of kind into sub-competitions (P087: competition on θ; competition on $\min\{d_1, d_2\}$); (2) impose candidate explicit policies; (3) derive the barrier as the closed-form separating surface (P087 eq. (19): $\cos\theta = \frac{(1-a^2)(d_1+d_2)^2}{2d_1d_2} - 1$; P112 Theorems 1–2: RPE integration from the BUP, then "bond" the two sub-game barriers, switching at $(\tilde x, \tilde y)$); (4) partition the state space into zones with zone-specific optimal strategies (P087 four zones, eqs. (44)–(65), including rendezvous (46)–(49) and angle-reduction strategies). Guarantee: **SP within zones** [proven for the barrier itself; the zone-strategy optimality is argued via "the concept of Nash equilibrium" (P087 Theorem 4.1 proof step iv) — semi-formal]. Switch/stay conditions are geometric equalities (e.g., $r_{12}(t_0) \le a(d_1 + d_2)$, P087 Thm 4.1).
- **Escape-angle territorial defense.** P056: attacker's escape angle inside the defender's territory; case split on territory size (ordinary/small/large) with constructive winning conditions and switching strategies for all three players (Theorems 4.1–4.7) [proven within the case analysis]; PMP layer (constant costates) justifies straight-line segments.
- **Orthogonal-line and max-type barriers (equal speed).** P096 eq. (18): $\mathcal{B} = (W^A)^2 - (W^D)^2$ (squared distances to the closest area point); 2v1 $\mathcal{B} = \max\{\mathcal{B}_1, \mathcal{B}_2\}$ (eq. (23)); capture-type decision rule via signed side tests $\Phi_1, \Phi_2$ (eq. (28)) — cooperative simultaneous capture at $\mathbf{I}^*_{k2}$ iff $\Phi_1 < 0 \wedge \Phi_2 < 0$ [asserted — no formal proof the rule selects the team-optimal capture type; degenerate case $\Gamma = 0$ unhandled]. P035's $B = \max\{B_1, B_2\}$ is the same pattern proved as a barrier [proven].

## 4. Enclosure, relay, herding, and covering strategies

- **Enclosure-region pursuit (ERP).** P105 Defs. 1–3: maintain a dynamically updated compact strictly convex region containing the evader with positive distance from $\Omega_{\text{goal}}$; computed by **forward** optimization (steer-to-ERP problem (7)) instead of backward HJI; coalition reduction — at most two pursuers matter per evader (Theorem 1, support-constraint counting à la Calafiore–Campi) [proven]; 1-vs-1/2-vs-1 subgame outcomes lifted to a receding-horizon allocation. Guarantee: **FWD** (winning certificate), Dubins-car pursuers.
- **Relay pursuit.** P070: active pursuer = argmin of a least-time metric; round-pursuit law (eqs. (7)–(8)) steers *perpendicular to the LOS* until co-linearity, letting the static teammate capture; switching regions carved by Voronoi lines, Apollonius circles, and a new Apollonius-like circle; sufficient capture condition (Prop. 2, eq. (16)) [proven]; behavioral asymmetry exploited: the evader is assumed to see only the active pursuer (Assumption 1, Remark 1). P077: relay by least $\varphi_c$ with the safe/failing/risky partition and quickest-descent switching (eqs. (4)–(9)) [proven]; capture radius ℓ; log-risk metric drives evasion. Guarantee: **FWD**; input: positions + closed-form times.
- **Herding / attacker relocation.** P014: defenders form a virtual channel around a Dubins-smoothed A* guide path; decomposition into path-defense game (herder vs attacker on $L_h$) and path-blockade game (escorts vs attacker on walls); barriers in (ρ, β) polar coordinates from relative-distance/LOS-angle sensing only [proven]; Game of Kind on the extended target set. Guarantee: **SP for subgames**; information: relative only (no absolute positions).
- **Covering by projected dominance (3D, slower defenders).** P013: N slower UAVs vs one faster intruder; 3D Apollonius sphere (Def. 2, eqs. (2)–(5)); tangent-cone angle $\sin\theta = \lambda$ position-independent (Lemma 1, eq. (6) [proven]); central projection of the sphere onto the goal plane is an **ellipse** (Theorem 1, eqs. (13)–(16) [proven]); lower-altitude UAVs cover the guarded area with joint projected ellipses via **weighted Voronoi partitions** (eqs. (25)–(33)); CBF-QCQP layer (eqs. (46)–(49)) for safety. Guarantee: **FWD/coverage** — cooperative capture guarantee under the covering condition; heuristic elsewhere.
- **Encirclement via k-WTA + potential fields.** P084: Predator-Pack model (eqs. (11)–(19)) with Gaussian encirclement modulation $\varphi_{ik} = \exp\{-c_P[(R_i - d_a)^2 + (R_k - d_a)^2]\}$ turning pairwise repulsion into uniform angular spacing; capture = k pursuers inside detection range with adjacent subtended angles above threshold (100° for k = 3) [asserted — not derived from reachability]. Guarantee: **H**.

## 5. Assignment + local-game two-layer architectures

The library's dominant multiplayer pattern (P083's "two levels", P104's workflow): solve many-vs-one games of kind for barriers/winning regions, then match coalitions to evaders combinatorially.

- **Maximum matching (MM).** P083 Item 8 / eq. (3): bipartite graph with edges where the intruder lies in the defender's 1v1 winning region; $Q^* \le Q_{MM} = N_A - N_{MM}$ (upper bound); origin Chen–Zhou–Tomlin [proven for the bound]. Guarantee: **bound only**; ignores cooperation.
- **Maximum independent set (MIS).** P083 Item 9 / eq. (4): conflict graph over assignments; $Q^* \le Q_{MIS} \le Q_{MM}$; NP-hard [proven hardness, loose bound].
- **Local Game Regions (LGR).** P015 / P083 Items 10–12: $Q_{LG} = \max_G \sum_{k \in G} \max\{q^k, 0\}$ over disjoint LGRs; intruder strategy (Theorem 1, eq. (7)) guarantees $Q^* \ge Q_{LG}$ against any defense [proven]; defender policy = remove uncapturable intruders, occupy LGRs, then 1v1; equilibrium sandwich eq. (7) for the class of configurations treated in [28] [proven, scoped]; computed in $O(N_D^4)$ as max-weight independent set on a circular-arc graph. The only two-sided (upper+lower) machinery in the perimeter-defense line. Empirics: Q_MIS > Q_LG in 0–16% of trials growing with N_A; LGR tracks the ~$N_A^5$ trend (P015 Fig. 9).
- **Coalition ILP (2^N coalitions → execution coalitions).** P100 Theorem 5 / eq. (50): 0–1 IP over coalition variables with pursuer-disjointness constraints (A3 built by Algorithm 2); Lemma 5: any winning coalition degenerates to a ≤ 2-pursuer subcoalition ⇒ only execution coalitions needed; q = exact number of guaranteed captures [proven, scoped to the barrier analysis]; mirror property (Lemma 4) handles pursuers starting inside $\Omega_{tar}$; Algorithm 1 finds active pursuers in $O(n_k^2)$.
- **MBMC + Sequential Matching (3D, radii).** P103: bipartite graph (28) + conflict graph (29) + BIP (30); NP-hard via 3-dimensional matching (Theorem 4.1 [proven]); Sequential Matching Algorithm 1 with 1/3-approximation (1/2 without 3-pursuer coalitions; exact without 2- and 3-coalitions) (Theorem 4.2 [proven]); APX-complete (Corollary 2). P104 Sec. 5.2 records the same 1/N_c-approximation pattern generically.
- **Two-layer with heterogeneous costs and makespan.** P054: allocation costs are game-derived capture times $T_{ij}$ (Theorem 1 isochron values); bottleneck objective; distributed Hungarian + binary search (Theorem 5) — see `algorithm_design.md` §2. Guarantee: **exact per group** [proven, with the δ_i-synchronization caveat flagged].
- **Hedonic (bottom-up, no local games).** P045: no capturability layer at all; agents self-organize by SPAO preferences; Nash stable partition exists and is reached in ≤ $|A|(|A|+1)/2$ iterations [proven, counting informality flagged]; suboptimality bounds Theorems 3–4 [proven]. Guarantee: **stability + a posteriori computable bound**, not game-theoretic capture.
- **Matching-based CTF** (P089): reduction to 2D-1A/1D-1A; joint two-phase defender strategy; MBMC approximate sequential matching. **LLM layer** (P059): analytic knowledge (winning $K_w$, reduction $K_r$, constraints $K_c$) as prompts; two-chain planning; analytic corrections remove unreasonable pairs/redundant pursuers/physical violations (Algorithms 2–4) — guarantee inherited from the analytic layer, the LLM is a search prior [asserted].

## 6. PMP-derived open-loop solutions (two-sided or one-sided)

- **Canonical recipe** (P044 Theorem 1 workflow; P052 Lemma 1): write H; stationarity gives controls in terms of costates; $\dot\lambda = -\partial H/\partial x = 0$ for integrator dynamics ⇒ constant costates ⇒ straight lines; solve the terminal-boundary algebra. Guarantee: **NC** (necessary conditions); saddle-point status requires a value verification (P044 Theorem 2 performs it for 2P1E [proven]).
- **Reduced TPBVP with adjoint antisymmetry.** P108: $\lambda^P + \lambda^E = 0$ (eq. (18)) collapses 24 → 12 states; Theorem 1: equivalent 6-state/6-adjoint game with 4 free unknowns $(\lambda^r_f, \tau_f)$ (eq. (29)) [proven in the original⇒reduced direction only; **converse asserted** — no Isaacs/value check, archive §5.2]. Controls: pursuer componentwise bang-bang $u^P = -\rho^P\mathrm{sign}(\lambda^v)$ (cube set), evader saturated alignment $u^E = \rho^E\lambda^v/\|\lambda^v\|$ (ball set) — the discontinuity is what breaks the sensitivity-ODE Jacobian (Remark 1) and motivates finite differences.
- **Quartic-closed-form TAD.** P022: aimpoint $I^*$ on the orthogonal bisector of AD, abscissa the real root of quartic (15); Theorems 1–2 give closed-form saddle headings for DT ≤ AT and DT > AT; Value (17)/(74) C¹ over the escape region, HJI-verified [proven]; Corollary 1 covers the degenerate T heading. This is the strongest **SP** guarantee in the TAD class.
- **Average-speed-ratio reduction.** P055: freeze $\bar\alpha = \tilde v_D/\tilde v_A$ at maximum; PMP solves force allocation (bang-bang then coast); 1-D extremal search over the terminal value; Apollonius machinery re-instantiated ((47)–(49) re-instantiate (14)–(15); (68) offsets the barrier by $r_T$) [proven under the coast assumptions; ratio-freezing is the structural assumption].
- **Bang-bang yaw penetration.** P107: Stage 1 converts NPC→PAC via a bang-bang yaw law (eq. (42)) with sufficient conditions (31)/(39) on speed ratio and radius-to-yaw-gain ratio; KKT-based penetration condition (eq. (20)) [proven]; indirect-point formulas flagged as non-derivable as printed (archive §5).

## 7. HJI / viscosity value-function solutions (solve-or-verify)

Two usages (P104 Sec. 3.2): HJI as a **solver** (numerical, small state) and as a **verifier** of candidate closed forms (three-condition certificate: C¹ on X∖M, HJI on X∖M, V = Φ on ∂M — P104 Sec. 3.2 items 1–3; P044 Theorem 1 cites Hespanha).

- **Verified closed forms (SP, strongest).** P022 (TAD, quartic aimpoint); P034 (CTF Phases I–II; Phase I Value (17) fully proved; Phase II (25) C¹ except dispersal surface {y_A = y_D}); P038 (CTF attack/transition/retreat, including the constrained aimpoint (25)–(32) and transition hyperbola (57)); P035 (3D 2P1E, Value (13)/(22)); P019 (two cooperative attackers — unique viscosity solution with the m-branch singular-surface Lemma 1 (eq. (2)) [proven]; the library's only multi-branch value proof); P080 (TAD r > 0 for A only; Value (16) HJI-proven); P082 (both radii > 0; three PMP cases by T's placement relative to the D–A interception hyperbola; Values (16)/(46) verified); P049 (reconnaissance Phase I verified against HJI, eqs. (57)–(59)); P031 (geometric solution justified as HJI solution for arbitrary convex targets, r > 0, arbitrary n — Theorem 2.1 [proven]); P050 (value = viscosity solution everywhere, C¹ exactly where LICQ holds — Theorems 1–2 [proven]); P096 (1v1/2v1 Values $\mathcal{B}^2/(4\Theta^2)$ verified along optimal play; **HJI verified only for the reduced point-mass game — filtered/underactuated execution is not covered**, archive Proof gap 2).
- **Viscosity/level-set solvers (numerical).** P039/P040: constrained HJI reach-avoid sets; upper value as viscosity solution of the constrained terminal-value HJI (P040 eqs. (12)–(15), Evans–Souganidis representation; Isaacs condition (16)); rule violations encoded as opponent winning conditions; sequential composition $\tilde{R}_A = \mathcal{G}_F \cap R_A$ (P039 eq. (5)). P069-style isochrone methods replace grids when obstacles are polygonal. Complexity caution: P103 Remark 3.2 — a 12-state subgame needs ~$10^{12}$ grid points at 10 pts/dim; P083 p. 481: numerical HJI needs a stored look-up table per speed ratio.
- **Parametric-optimization verification (alternative to PDE grids).** P048 Theorem 2 / P050: associate V with the optimal value of a parametric program (P); SOSC (P048) or LICQ (P050) ⇒ classical C¹ value satisfying the Isaacs/HJI equation; P050 additionally handles nondifferentiability via Clarke subdifferentials (Theorem 1(2), eq. (27)) [proven]. P021: Danskin-based state-gradient machinery ((30), (31), (36), (37)) certifies equilibrium via the Isaacs condition for the CTF quasi-convex program.

## 8. Optimization-based strategies (convex / quasi-convex programs as feedback)

- **Dominance-region projection (convex).** P104 eq. (19) / P103 / P050 eq. (36): $\min \|x - y\|^2$ s.t. $f_{ij}(x) \ge 0$, $g(y) \le 0$ — solved online as a QCQP; P050 runs it per sampling step in a 12-D state ("far out of reach for real-time HJI", archive Sec. 6). Guarantee: **FWD** (P103: HJI-equals-convex-program reduction via KKT–Isaacs — the KKT conditions of the program are the Isaacs conditions of the game [proven]).
- **Quasi-convex two-phase CTF.** P021: phase-2 value $g^*_\mathcal{R}$ convex (Theorem 1); CTF value $W^*$ quasi-convex (19) (Theorems 11–12); unique minimum whenever $W^* > V^{(2)}$ (Theorem 14); iterative two-step Algorithm 1 with analytical Step-1 projection for rhombus flags. Guarantee: **SP** (value certified) [proven].
- **Ellipse certificate for two-phase play.** P018 Theorems 1–4: ellipse iso-cost sets, minimax characterization via value functions $h_i$ (eq. (7)) + Danskin; uniform-minimizer structural assumption reduces the game of kind to a scalar condition [proven under the assumption].
- **Chance-constrained belief inflation.** P027: virtual evader with Gaussian perturbation; chance-constrained trajectory prediction; IBR + distributed ADMM; only formal guarantee is Lemma 1 (probabilistic feasibility of the scenario solution, eqs. (20)–(21)) — **no NE existence or IBR/ADMM convergence proofs** (archive §5; iterations capped by Remark 1). Guarantee: **H with a sampling certificate**.
- **Level-k receding-horizon behavioral play.** P029: double integrators; receding-horizon program (4); recursive level-k best responses (5)–(9) (convergent infinite-level play ⇔ NE); Bayesian belief update over rationality levels θ^t(k) (eqs. (10)–(12)); one-step trajectory-difference inference is non-identifying in free motion — hence the Bayesian scoring of predicted trajectories [asserted for the mechanism; the nesting (7)–(9) is analytic]. Guarantee: **NC/H** (nonequilibrium by design).

## 9. Learning-based strategies (what is trained, what is guaranteed)

Catalog of the RL family with reward/state design and **missing guarantees** (all **H** unless noted):

- **Minimax Q-learning on a reduced matrix game** (P078): (N+1)-player non-zero-sum game reduced to a per-stage two-player zero-sum matrix game (pursuer group = one minimizer); payoff entries from closed-form time metrics $\phi_c, \phi_a, \phi_s, \phi_T$ (eq. (5)); LP pair (6)–(7) for mixed strategies; invariant 2×4 feature abstraction (p1 relay / p2 all-active vs e1–e4 including largest-gap bisector e2); state ψ = sigmoid-squashed time ratios (eq. (12)) — player-count invariant, enabling cross-size transfer; minimax Q fixed point (eq. (17)) with linear approximation; reward (18)–(21) = heading derivative + safety-ratio increase + terminal ±1. **Missing: no convergence theorem** (linear-approx minimax QL is not guaranteed convergent; the paper only says "iteratively approximate", archive §5.6); off-policy ε-greedy behavior unanalyzed; while-condition typo ("or" should be "and", p. 9 step 3). Sensitivity finding: ℓ/(v_eΔt) is the true dimensionless training parameter (p. 12).
- **QMIX with Apollonius-partition shaping** (P097): geometry enters in three places — reward $U_i = -[(1-\delta_i)\|\Delta x_i\| + S_t]$ with $S_t$ = dominant-area area (eq. (17), effectively all $U_i = -S_t$), role mask (Algorithm 1 neighbor identification + freezing eq. (21), implementing Lemma 3 redundancy persistence), scripted fixation evader (Algorithm 2, Theorem 1 aimpoints on circle intersections eqs. (15)–(16) [proven geometrically]); NE existence via ordinal potential game (Theorem 2, $\Phi = -nS_t$) [proven **for pursuer deviations with frozen neighbor set** — evader clamped to best response]; finite-time capture via CSCT monotonicity (Theorem 3) — **proof assumes line-of-sight learned play** ($\theta_e = \theta_i$), i.e., silently assumes the learned policy is pure pursuit (archive gaps i–iv). Termination by area threshold $S_t$, **never linked formally** to the CSCT criterion; hyperparameters/capture threshold unreported.
- **Game-theory-assisted MAPPO (GT early termination).** P017: Phase-II Nash payoff $J^* = \max\{0, c_y - r\}$ (Theorem 1, eq. (16) [analytic, defender-side converse inherited not proven]) or $\min_{p \in cl(R_A)} p_y$ via a small convex program (Prop. 1, eqs. (19)–(20) [asserted by analogy, no proof]); at sensing time $t_s$ the episode terminates and all defenders receive $R = J^*(x(t_s))$ (Def. 4, Algorithm 1) — pursuit is never simulated; undiscounted payoff equivalence (eqs. (21)–(22)) stated [asserted]; discounted returns differ by design (earlier sensing valued more); for suboptimal pursuit execution the GT reward is an upper bound ⇒ deployment can underperform training (archive §5.6). Savings heuristic $T_p \approx [(\rho_s - \rho_c)/(v_D - v_A)]/\Delta t$ (Prop. 2 — heuristic). Result: +10% (1v1), +18.9% (3v1) mean reward, better sensing configurations (Fig. 7); 3 seeds, no significance tests.
- **Curriculum MATD3 for impulsive spacecraft TAD** (P111): HMATD3 = MATD3 + "Hamiltonian regularization" loss on value networks (eqs. (18)–(27)) — heuristic loss shaping, **no convergence/optimality proof**; performance-driven curriculum adapts the maneuver interval Δt online via a staircase map of a sliding-window truncated rate (eqs. (28)–(30)) — note Assumption 1(3) (fixed Δt) holds within an episode only; dual-component reward (process + terminal, eqs. (31)–(35)); sun-elevation constraint handling rotates the restricted vector and corrects the impulse $\Delta\bar{v}_l = \mu_v\Delta v_{adj,l} + \Delta v_l$, $\mu_v = 0.2$ (eq. (38)) — **no feasibility check** that the correction respects Δv_max. Platform: RTX 1060, ~10 h training.
- **Hybrid classical-guidance + PPO** (P003): control blend $u_{hybrid} = \omega\hat{u}_{classic} + (1 - \omega)\hat{u}_{RL}$ (eq. (5)) with ω annealed 1→0; three-phase speed-ratio curriculum (0.7×→0.9×→1.1×); classical bridge term in the reward; adversarial self-play with role-encoded flocks. **Analytic gap recorded**: the intercept "solver" eqs. (1)–(4) is a least-squares time-of-closest-approach, not a guaranteed collision time (archive verification); feasibility failure (negative t) acknowledged by authors.
- **CTDE MA-POCA pursuit with adversarial co-evolution** (P072): 6-DOF USV in Unity3D; POMDP with task-specific action spaces (eq. (11)); shaped dense rewards encoding simultaneous-arrival encirclement (eqs. (19)–(26)); MA-POCA counterfactual credit (eqs. (27)–(32)); PPO+BC+GAIL escape policies; curriculum + adversarial evolution against overfitting. Guarantee: **H**; also documents a printed rotation-matrix typo (Eq. (2)).
- **Hierarchical mean-field MARL** (P110): $Q^k = Q^k_{LOC} + Q^k_{IMF}$ factorization (eq. (6)); group mean-field collapse $Q_{GRP} = \sum_k \lambda_k Q^k_{LOC} + 2Q_{GMF}(\mu(x))$ (eq. (11)) under additive virtual rewards + first-order Taylor [derived, eqs. (7)–(11)]; execution on local observations only. Guarantee: **H** (approximation argument, no game-theoretic bound).
- **CBF-safeguarded ADP saddle** (P046): nominal saddle strategies $u_j^{n*} = -\lambda_j\tanh(\frac{1}{2\lambda_j}R_j^{-1}B^\top\nabla V^*)$ (eq. ## 10. Strategy-regime classifications (where the archives classify, and the RC/CC/SR question)

A literal "RC/CC/SR" taxonomy does not appear verbatim in the archived papers; the classifications the library actually uses are:

- **Safe / failing / risky partition** (P077 Prop. 1): state space split by comparisons of closed-form times $\varphi_s, \varphi_c, \varphi_T$; circular boundaries $S_e(x_i), F_e(x_i)$ (Prop. 2) — the closest "SR"-style object (safe region) with a robust variant in P028 (RESR, Theorem 1) and the virtual-evader safe region of P027.
- **Relay vs cooperative capture-type decision** (P096 eq. (28) $\Phi_1/\Phi_2$ side tests; P015's 2v1 $R_C$ vs naive $R_I$; P084's k-WTA team sizes) — the "CC" (cooperative capture) analogue; P099's Remarks 5–6 make the OTP/peak test finite (≤ 3 candidate points at α = 1).
- **Outcome-scenario classifications**: P053's 16-case taxonomy (Table I, p. 7) by superiority class × tangency timing; P012's two-stage/three-subgame circle-relation classification; P091's Scenarios 1–3 by speed-ratio sign; P087's four zones; P056's territory-size cases; P041/P106's obstacle-visibility cases. When writing a paper, prefer naming your cases after these precedents rather than importing RC/CC/SR labels.

## 11. Selection guidance (which pattern for which paper claim)

1. Claiming a saddle-point value in ≤ 6-D simple motion → P022/P034/P035/P019/P082-style closed form + HJI verification (three-condition certificate, P104 Sec. 3.2).
2. Claiming multiplayer guarantees without a value → barrier/dominance FWD certificate + matching layer (P100/P103/P015), or ERP forward analysis (P105).
3. Nonzero capture radii + heterogeneous speeds → function-based dominance $f_{ij}$ (P103/P104 Def. 3) + convex program (P050 eq. (36)); Cartesian ovals replace circles (P031/P060/P094).
4. Nonholonomic or underactuated players → heading-reference decomposition (P047), guidance-point reduction + CBF-QP (P096), ERP (P105); do not claim the point-mass value transfers unless verified (P096 Proof gap 2).
5. Partial information → two-phase deployment/capture decomposition (P062), conservative radius-free barriers (P064), fuzzy-region vocabulary (P067), RESR (P028), or analytic-terminal-value RL (P017).
6. Learning component → state the missing guarantee explicitly (P078/P097/P111 have none; P046's invariance and P085's VI convergence are the learnable guarantees available); hybrid analytic-terminal rewards (P017) are the library's best practice for guaranteed continuations.
